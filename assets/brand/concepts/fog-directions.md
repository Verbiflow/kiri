# Fog directions

Concept exploration generated with the built-in image generation tool. Supersedes the chrome object direction. Not final favicon or desktop exports.

Kiri written as 霧 means fog or mist: https://www.japandict.com/%E9%9C%A7?lang=en

01 Clearing, 02 Mist bands, 03 Veil, 04 Convergence, 05 Horizon.

Design intent: strong flat silhouettes, visible square-pixel dithering, and clarity emerging from fog. Production work should simplify texture at favicon sizes and remove soft halos from the selected concept.

## Generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand.
Design a sophisticated new identity concept board for KIRI, an AI-native developer Git application. Name concept: Japanese 霧, mist/fog. This is a fresh design, not related to any previous chrome objects.
Produce FIVE thoughtful distinct abstract flat logo concepts in a beautifully composed 5-column horizontal contact sheet, 2400x1000-ish wide aspect ratio, uniform solid near-black #101113 background. Each column has one large silver-white symbol at top, the same small lowercase wordmark "kiri" in refined neutral geometric sans below, then a tiny number 01 through 05. Generous negative space, brand-studio restraint. No tiles, no product renderings.
CRITICAL VISUAL LANGUAGE: authentic old-computer 1-bit ORDERED BAYER DITHERING. Highly visible sharp square pixels in regular 2x2 and 4x4 checkerboard patterns. Pixel units around 3-5 pixels on the output, not fine random grain. All tonal gradients must resolve into binary light/dark pixel grids. Solid white shapes transition through structured checkerboard gray into black. Think classic Macintosh bitmap graphics and Japanese experimental graphic design. It must visibly be a bitmap design, not a photoreal metal image, not soft blur. Restrained metallic feel ONLY from stepped light/dark bands in the flat artwork. Pixel texture is essential and must be conspicuous.
Five concepts:
01 CLEARING: a bold near-square cloud of ordered dither with a sharply cut black vertical rectangular corridor in its middle, ending just below the top; a little white horizontal ledge at its base. The clear negative space emerging from mist hints at a developer workspace without drawing a literal monitor. Compact, balanced, striking.
02 MIST BANDS: three thick short horizontal white bars of slightly different lengths, staggered into a compact square silhouette. Each solid bar dissolves toward the right into regular Bayer checkerboard pixels. The bars feel like low fog banks or lines of a diff. A clean very simple iconic mark, no motion blur.
03 VEIL: one bold circular white disc, its lower half interrupted by two crisp horizontal black channels. White portions of the circle dissolve into coarse ordered dither downwards, suggesting a moon obscured by fog. Strong round silhouette, no landscape.
04 CONVERGENCE: two compact flat offset fields of ordered pixel fog, left and right, that resolve into one crisp narrow white vertical slab at their center. Abstract symmetry, digital clarity emerging from uncertainty. No arrows, no chevrons.
05 HORIZON: one compact rounded-square solid white frame, thick upper edge, bottom half dissolving into conspicuous black/white checkerboard pixels. A single thin white horizontal horizon cuts across the empty black center. No perspective or extrusion.
All marks flat, monochrome, optically balanced, each around 260px wide with space between. Large visible bitmap pattern. Avoid ALL 3D, bevels, chrome ribbons, floating objects, leaves, stacked slabs, glossy app tiles, lens effects, film grain, stippling, random spray noise, gradients without dithering, Git branch diagrams, letters inside the marks, AI sparkles, mountains, torii gates, Japanese text, ornamental complexity.
Text exactly "kiri" beneath every symbol and numbers "01" "02" "03" "04" "05". Editorial precision, calm and technical.
