# Source artwork

Generated with the built-in image generation tool from approved concept 04. The opaque stencil is the input to the production vector export. Exported SVGs contain paths, not embedded images.

## Prompt

Production source for vector tracing. Reproduce ONLY symbol 04, fourth in reference, the Kiri Convergence mark. Standalone centered symbol. Solid pure black #000000 background everywhere, entirely opaque. Mark PURE WHITE #ffffff only. Exactly two colors, white foreground on black background. NO transparency, NO checkerboard background, NO gray, NO lighting, NO texture, NO glow, NO shadow, NO blur. Crisp perfectly flat stencil.
Maintain selected shape: central solid vertical white rectangular bar flanked by two symmetric wings of square-pixel ordered dither. Wings expand at outer upper/lower edges and narrow where they meet the middle. Approximately hourglass negative-space silhouette around the central bar. Pixel squares must be uniform and aligned on a rigid grid, 16 to 24 pixels per square, spaced in intentional Bayer checkerboard patterns, with sparse isolated outer squares fading to black. Central bar 80 pixels wide and 480 high within a 1254-square composition. Overall symbol approximately 880 wide and 850 high centered, generous black margins.
No text, no numbers, no other symbols, no tile, no perspective. This is flat 1-bit bitmap source art for the approved identity, not a reinterpretation.
